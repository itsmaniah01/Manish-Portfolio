About

Built on discipline, moved into people-facing work.

I started on a manufacturing line, not in an office — inspecting electronic assemblies at Salcomp Manufacturing and later Lava International, where a defect that slipped through wasn't an abstract risk, it was a real product going out the door. That work trained me to check against the spec every time, no exceptions.

A short stint as a supervisor at Exide Manufacturing gave me my first taste of managing people and outcomes, not just parts. I carried that instinct for accuracy and ownership straight into my current role as a Loan Officer at Bharat Financial Inclusion, where the "spec" is now a customer's financial need, and getting it right matters just as much.

I'm looking for an executive-level opportunity where that same combination — dependable process discipline plus genuine care for the person on the other side of the desk — can keep growing.
